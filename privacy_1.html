Privacy Policy

We build SynthNerd for humans, not trackers. Here's exactly what we collect — and what we don't.

Last updated: January 8, 2025

TL;DR

No cookies. No cross-site tracking. No creepy profiles. We use Plausible Analytics (cookie-free) to see basic traffic, and we disclose affiliate links. That's it.

No Tracking Cookies

SynthNerd does not use tracking cookies, fingerprinting, or third-party ad pixels. We don't sell your data, and we don't buy data about you. We don't need it.

Essential, first-party cookies are only used if you log in or set preferences (currently: none). If that changes, we'll update this page.

Analytics — Plausible

We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-friendly, EU-hosted alternative to Google Analytics. It's cookie-free, doesn't collect personal data, and doesn't track you across sites.

  • Measures: page views, referrers, countries (approximate), devices — all aggregated
  • No IP storage, no personal identifiers, no cross-site tracking
  • Data is retained for 12 months, then auto-deleted

Affiliate Links Disclosure

Some links on SynthNerd are affiliate links. If you click and buy, we may earn a commission — at no extra cost to you. We only recommend tools we actually test and use.

Affiliate programs may set their own first-party cookies when you visit a merchant site. We don't control that, but we mark affiliate links clearly across the site.

Newsletter

Our newsletter is optional. If you subscribe, we store only your email address (via our email provider) to send you updates. No profiling, no sharing, no selling.

  • You can unsubscribe anytime with one click
  • We send max 2 emails per week, usually 1
  • We don't track opens with invisible pixels

Ask anything about privacy
